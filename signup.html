Become a Registered Life Saver

Joining our national voluntary blood network takes less than 2 minutes and puts you on standby to save lives during hospital emergencies in your neighborhood.

General Donor Eligibility:
  • Age between 18 and 65 years
  • Minimum weight of 45-50 kg
  • Hemoglobin level ≥ 12.5 g/dl
  • No major surgery in past 6 months
  • Gap of at least 90 days from last donation
Your personal contact number is kept safe & strictly used for emergency medical cross-matching.
