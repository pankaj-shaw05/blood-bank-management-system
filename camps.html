Host a Blood Donation Camp

LifeStream partners with universities, tech parks, housing societies, and NGOs to conduct safe mobile blood drives.

1. Request a Date & Venue

Choose a well-ventilated hall or auditorium. Submit your booking request at least 7 to 10 days in advance.

2. Full Medical Logistics Provided

We arrive with medical beds, sterile single-use kits, licensed phlebotomists, and a refrigerated transport vehicle.

3. Refreshments & Certificates

All voluntary donors receive nutritious post-donation snacks, juices, digital appreciation certificates, and donor cards.
