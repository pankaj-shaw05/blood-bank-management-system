Become a Registered Life Saver

Joining our national voluntary blood network takes less than 2 minutes and puts you on standby to save lives during hospital emergencies in your neighborhood.

General Donor Eligibility:
  • Age between 18 and 65 years
  • Minimum weight of 45–50 kg
  • Hemoglobin level ≥ 12.5 g/dl
  • No major surgery in past 6 months
  • Gap of at least 90 days from last donation
Hospital Facility Guidelines:
  • Verified healthcare facility within East Singhbhum
  • 24/7 emergency admission & blood intake
  • Direct integration with district dispatch grid
  • Authorized medical representative sign-off
  • Instant emergency requisition broadcast
Your personal contact number is kept safe & strictly used for emergency medical cross-matching.

Terms & Medical Ethics Policy
National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC) & Drugs and Cosmetics Act Compliance
1. Voluntary Non-Remunerated Blood Donation

All blood donations facilitated via LifeStream are strictly voluntary, humanitarian, and non-commercial. Selling or trading blood units is illegal under Indian law and strictly forbidden on this platform.

2. Donor Eligibility & Safety Protocol

Donors must be aged 18–65 years, weight ≥ 45 kg, with hemoglobin ≥ 12.5 g/dL. A mandatory deferral period of 90 days for males and 120 days for females between whole blood donations must be honored.

3. Accredited Healthcare Facilities Only

Only verified healthcare institutions and certified blood storage units located within the designated East Singhbhum district boundary may requisition units or manage reserves through this portal.

4. Data Privacy & Credential Protection

All donor personal telemetry and contact credentials are encrypted. Contact details are only shared during verified critical or emergency requisitions to protect privacy.
