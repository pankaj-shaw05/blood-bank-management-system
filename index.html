Make An Impact

Why Voluntary Blood Donation Matters

A single donation of whole blood takes only 10 to 15 minutes and can save up to three individual lives.

Save 3 Lives

Your whole blood donation is separated into Red Blood Cells, Platelets, and Plasma, treating trauma, dengue, and surgery patients.

Free Health Screening

Every donor undergoes complimentary checks for blood pressure, pulse, hemoglobin, and infectious markers before donating.

Replenish Blood Cells

Donation stimulates bone marrow to produce fresh, new blood cells within weeks, promoting cardiovascular wellness.

Donor Recognition

Receive digital donor cards, certified appreciation badges, and priority assistance should you or your family ever need blood.
